BAKAINA - FACEBOOK AD POSTER PROMPTS
=====================================

HOW TO USE
1. Use ChatGPT (image), Gemini, Ideogram or Leonardo.
2. UPLOAD YOUR REAL PRODUCT PHOTO as a reference image every time, and add:
   "Use the uploaded product exactly as it looks: same shape, colour, strap and display."
3. Size: 4:5 portrait (1080 x 1350) for Facebook/Instagram feed.
   For Stories/Reels make a second version at 9:16 (1080 x 1920).
4. The image comes out WITHOUT text. Add the Bangla text in Canva
   (font: "Noto Serif Bengali" or "Hind Siliguri", colours #C8305F pink and #2B2620 dark).
5. Check the display on the belt: it must not show a wrong number. If it does, cover it in Canva.

PASTE THIS AT THE END OF EVERY PROMPT (style lock):
soft blush-pink and warm ivory palette, gentle diffused natural light, premium minimal
skincare-brand advertising style, photorealistic, high detail, clean composition,
no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, 4:5 portrait

---------------------------------------------------
META AD RULES - READ BEFORE YOU PUBLISH
---------------------------------------------------
Facebook rejects or restricts health ads that talk TO the person about their body.
  NOT allowed:  "আপনার কি পিরিয়ডে ব্যথা হয়?"   "Do you suffer from cramps?"
                "আপনার ব্যথা দূর করবে"   "Cures period pain"
  Allowed:      "পিরিয়ডের দিনগুলোয় আরামের উষ্ণতা"   "আরাম"   "উষ্ণতা"   "স্বস্তি"
Say comfort/warmth, never cure/treat/heal. No before/after.
Use only real prices (৳৮৯৯, was ৳১,৬৯০). No fake countdowns or "only 3 left".
